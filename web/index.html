Welcome to OpenCraft

            

            

              

                A world of blocks to mine, reshape and slowly turn into a factory. There are no enemies and no rush:
                play at your own pace, alone or with friends.
              

              
Your first minutes

              
    
                
  1. Click Play. Look around with the mouse, walk with WASD and jump with Space.
    2. 
                
  2. Break something. Hold the left mouse button on a block. A tree is a good start.
    3. 
                
  3. Make something. Press E for your inventory and everything you can craft.
    4. 
                
  4. Follow the tips on the left of the screen. They lead you from your first tools to your first factory.
    5.

Esc brings you back here. Your world saves by itself in this browser. Every key is under Controls.

            

          


          

            

            

              Worlds are kept in this browser. Export one to keep a copy or to carry it to another computer. Creative worlds
              have everything researched and every item to hand.
            

          


          

            

            

              Up to four players share one world, run from the host's browser. Friends open your link in theirs: nothing to
              install. Some networks block direct connections; if joining fails, try another network.

Controls

            
Getting around

            

              
Mouse
Look around

              
W A S D
Walk

              
Space
Jump; in water, swim up

              
Shift
Sprint

              
C / Ctrl
Crouch (you won't walk off edges)

              
F
Fly on or off (Space and C rise and sink)

            

            
Building

            

              
Hold left mouse
Mine the block you point at

              
Right mouse
Place the selected block (belts run the way you face; drag to lay a line)

              
Right mouse on a box or machine
Open it (hold C to place against it instead)

              
R
Turn the belt or machine you point at

              
1–9 / wheel
Pick a hotbar slot

              
Q
Drop one item

            

            
Screens

            

              
E
Inventory and build menu

              
T
Research: choose what labs work on

              
M
Map: where you've been, your pins, where each ore lies

              
N
Minimap on or off

              
P
Power and production graphs

              
L
Blueprints

              
Hold Tab
Who is playing with you

            

            
Planning and helpers

            

              
B
Ghost mode: right-click plants a free ghost to build later

              
Z, Enter
In ghost mode: mark a box, then copy its machines as a blueprint

              
Y
Your personal drone fetches more of what you hold

              
U
Point the scanner at the next deposit

            

            
Camera

            

              
V
First person, third person or strategy view

              
G / Home
Free or follow camera; Home returns to your character

              
Strategy view
WASD pans, wheel zooms, right-click walks there, X stops

              
Ctrl + right mouse
Use or place in strategy view (within reach)

            

            
Other

            

              
H
Skip the tip on screen

              
K
Sound on or off

              
O
Sound designer

              
F3
Debug stats

              
Esc
Close a screen, free the mouse, then this menu
